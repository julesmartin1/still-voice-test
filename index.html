Voice test version 2

Makes a short story's audio on this phone, one piece at a time, saving as it goes. If the page crashes, it picks up where it stopped.

  1. Plug in the phone and use Wi-Fi. The first step downloads about 330 MB, and about 90 MB more if it needs its backup engine.
  2. In Settings › Display & Brightness › Auto-Lock, choose Never for now.
  3. Keep this page open and on screen.
  4. If Safari shows “A problem repeatedly occurred”, tap Reload. The test carries on.
